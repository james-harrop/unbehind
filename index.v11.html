How it works

Brief. Build. Hand over.

Step 1 · 30 minutes, free

Brief call

Tell me what you’d build if you knew how. Where you’re stuck. What good looks like. If it’s not a fit, I’ll say so before we hang up — no follow-up email, no chase.

Step 2 · 5-7 days, offline

Design and build

I go away and design the flow — the prompts, the integrations, the lot. I come back with a working prototype, recorded so you can see what’s happening.

We sit on Zoom and plumb it into your business. Test it. Fix what’s broken. You leave the call owning a working tool. On Partner or Embedded, this becomes a regular cadence — new flows, new fixes, every month.

What you’re probably thinking

The honest answers.

Why these prices?

Priced for owner-run businesses, deliberately. £500 one-off is half a freelancer day. £275/mo is Notion Pro + Linear + Calendly combined. £1,000/mo is one day a week of a London freelancer at consultant rate.

What if I don’t know what to build?

Bring the problem on the brief call. I’ll suggest flows you didn’t know were possible — inbox triage, lead scrapers, client reports, GPTs trained on your voice. The brief is the conversation; the build is the proof.

What if I’m completely non-technical?

Same as me twelve months ago. Claude Code writes the code in plain English; I do the design. You just bring the business knowledge — what you sell, who you sell to, what slows you down.

We’re a small team, not a solopreneur. Still for us?

Yes. The fit is owner-run, not headcount — if the person on the brief call owns the P&L, it works. Just you, or you and six staff. Embedded exists for exactly this.

Who owns the tools?

You do. Everything runs on your accounts and ships with Loom recordings and handover notes. Cancel any month and keep the lot.

Will it actually work in my business?

Probably. Brief call is free — if it’s not a fit, I’ll say so before we hang up. I’ve built for recruiters, consultants, coaches, agencies and service businesses. Mostly the bottleneck is repetitive work; AI does that well.
